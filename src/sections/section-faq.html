Is Wrong Ones available now?

Yes. Wrong Ones is live and available for download on the App Store and Google Play. Get the app, create an account, and start browsing and reporting incidents in your area today.

Is Wrong Ones a replacement for calling 911?

No. Wrong Ones does not replace 911 or any emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 first. Wrong Ones is for documenting incidents and revealing patterns in your area — awareness and prevention, not emergency response.

How do I get started?

Download Wrong Ones from the App Store or Google Play, sign up with your email, and you're in. Browsing the map is open right away; submitting a report just takes a free account.

Can I look up places outside my current location?

Yes. You can move the map anywhere and browse reports in any city, not just where you are right now. Search a city to see its reports, filter by incident type and date, and check an area before you visit or move there.

What types of incidents can I report?

You can report an abduction, an attempted abduction, or a suspicious event, and mark where it happened — a residence, a business, or a public place — along with the date, time, and a description of what occurred.

Will other users see who submitted a report?

No. Reports appear on the map without any reporter identity — no name, email, or profile is ever shown to other users. Exact locations are also generalized on the public map to protect everyone involved, while keeping the area-level pattern accurate.
