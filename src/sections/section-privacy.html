Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Wrong Ones (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides a community awareness app and related website that help people browse and report incidents in their area. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we may collect your email address and authentication details needed to sign you in and keep your account secure.

Reports you submit

If you submit a report, we collect the information you provide, such as incident type, approximate location, date, time, and description. Reports are shown on the public map without your name, email, or profile attached.

Location and usage data

With your permission, we may use device location to show nearby reports. We also collect limited technical and usage data (such as device type, app version, and interaction events) to operate, secure, and improve the Service.

Waitlist and website

If you join our waitlist or contact us through the website, we collect the email address and any information you choose to share so we can respond and send relevant updates.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide, maintain, and improve the Wrong Ones app and website;
  • display moderated reports and area-level patterns on the map;
  • create and manage accounts;
  • communicate about the Service, including important updates;
  • detect abuse, fraud, and safety issues; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

3. How reports appear on the map

Other users do not see who submitted a report. We do not publish your name, email, or profile with public map pins. Exact report locations may be generalized on the public map to help protect people involved while preserving useful area-level patterns.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us host, analyze, or operate the Service under contractual confidentiality obligations; when required by law or to protect rights and safety; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or similar corporate transaction.

5. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account subject to residual copies in backups and legal retention needs.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You may update account details in the app, control location permissions in your device settings, and unsubscribe from non-essential emails where offered. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request.

8. Children’s privacy

Wrong Ones is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. International users

If you access the Service from outside the country where we operate, your information may be processed in countries with different data-protection laws than your own.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at privacy@wrongones.app.
